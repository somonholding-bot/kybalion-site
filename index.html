Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything to use this service?

No. The service is free for consumers.

Are you an insurance company?

No. Kybalion provides an online consumer connection service. We are not an insurance carrier and do not issue policies. Licensed insurance professionals provide quotes and coverage.

Will I be contacted?

If you agree to be contacted, licensed insurance professionals may reach you by phone, text or email. You can opt out at any time on the opt-out page.

Will I get a lower price?

Quotes and savings vary by driver, vehicle and location and are not guaranteed.

How is my information used?

See our Privacy Policy for what we collect, who receives it and your choices.
