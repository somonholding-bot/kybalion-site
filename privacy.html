Privacy Policy

Who we are

Kybalion (kybalionus.com) operates an advertising and lead-generation service that connects consumers with licensed insurance professionals. We are not an insurer, agency or broker.

Information we collect

Information you provide in our form (ZIP code, vehicle details, coverage timing, driver age range, name, phone and email), technical data (IP address, browser, pages visited, date and time) and campaign data (such as UTM parameters). When you submit a request we also keep a record of the consent language shown to you, its version, the time, your IP address and the page URL.

How we use it

To process your request, connect you with licensed insurance professionals and their service providers, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations and improve our service.

Who we share it with

Only with the parties named in the consent you give at the time of submission and with service providers acting on our behalf. The list of those parties is shown next to the consent checkbox.

Your choices

You may opt out of calls, texts and emails and ask us not to sell or share your information on our opt-out page. For texts, reply STOP to any message. Depending on your state you may have rights to access, correct or delete your information.

Retention

We keep consent records for up to five years, or as long as our partners and the law require.

Security

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections and access is restricted.

Children

This service is not directed to anyone under 18.

Contact

Kybalion provides an online consumer connection service. We are not an insurance carrier and do not issue insurance policies.
